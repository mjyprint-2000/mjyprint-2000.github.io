Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes how MJY Autopost, the internal publishing tool of M.J.Y Print ("we", "us"), handles data.

What the app does

MJY Autopost uploads M.J.Y Print's own marketing images and short videos to our own official accounts only: our Facebook Page, Instagram professional account, YouTube channel and TikTok account. The app has no public sign-up and is used only by the business owner.

Data we access

  • OAuth access tokens for our own accounts, granted by us, used solely to publish our content and read basic account information (account name and ID).
  • For YouTube: the youtube.upload scope to upload our videos and set their thumbnails, and youtube.readonly to confirm the connected channel.
  • For TikTok: basic profile information and the permission to publish videos to our own TikTok account.

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users, followers or viewers.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the business's own computer and are never transmitted to any third party other than the platform they belong to.

Google API Services

MJY Autopost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the account settings of Facebook, Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or TikTok; stored tokens are then deleted. To request deletion or ask a question, contact us on WhatsApp at +20 109 677 3090 or via facebook.com/mjy.print.
