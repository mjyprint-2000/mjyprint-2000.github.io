About M.J.Y Print

M.J.Y Print is a printing and design shop in Agami, Alexandria, Egypt, producing personalised gifts: mugs, frames, glass clocks, T-shirts, certificates, stickers and educational boards.

MJY Autopost

MJY Autopost is M.J.Y Print's own internal publishing tool. It uploads our own marketing photos and short videos to our own official accounts only — our Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel and TikTok account — on a daily schedule. It is operated solely by the business owner, has no public sign-up, and does not collect, store or process data of any other users.
